Thanks to this year’s Sponsors

Our sponsors help build the financial foundation for the conference and the community, providing more opportunities for the community to build out the Python scientific computing tools and packages.

Institutional Sponsor

  • Enthought is a globally recognized leader in scientific software development with an unparalleled understanding of how to leverage advanced technologies to accelerate discovery and product development. We develop creative solutions grounded in AI and machine learning for companies ranging from start-ups to Fortune 500 in the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, materials science, chemistry, and semiconductor industries. Enthought R&D labs all over the world use Enthought Edge, our cloud-native enterprise platform purpose-built for scientific research. As creators of the original SciPy package and founders of the annual SciPy conference, Enthought has been an avid supporter and member of the open-source Python community for over 20 years.

Gold Sponsors

  • Covalent is an open source workflow orchestration platform designed to help users manage and execute tasks on high performance and quantum computing resources. Covalent is a cross-platform, cross-language pythonic tool and includes a browser-based user interface to visualize, monitor and reproduce workflows across a wide spectrum of hardware resources. Covalent is developed and maintained by the team at Agnostiq. You can learn more at www.covalent.xyz or by visiting their github at https://github.com/AgnostiqHQ/covalent.

  • Quansight is a data, science, and engineering firm that specializes in solving complex, data-related problems by leveraging the open source software at the foundation of innovation in AI and ML. Much of our technical staff are also contributors and maintainers of core PyData projects—including NumPy, PyTorch, SciPy, Jupyter, Nebari, Napari, Matplotlib, scikit-learn, Dask, conda, and Numba. Quansight was co-founded by Travis Oliphant, founder of Anaconda and NumFOCUS and the creator of SciPy, NumPy, and Numba.

  • Whether you're looking for compute power, database storage, content delivery, or other functionality, AWS has the services to help you build sophisticated applications with increased flexibility, scalability and reliability. When data is shared on AWS, anyone can analyze it and build services on top of it using a broad range of compute and data analytics products, including Amazon EC2, Amazon Athena, AWS Lambda, and Amazon EMR. Sharing data in the cloud lets data users spend more time on data analysis rather than data acquisition. The Registry of Open Data makes it easy to find datasets made publicly available through AWS services, where you can browse available data and learn how to register your own datasets.

  • Ansys is a global leader in engineering simulation software and services, bringing over 50 years of expertise to the table. Our comprehensive suite of simulation tools empowers organizations across industries like aerospace, automotive, electronics, healthcare, and manufacturing to predict product behavior, optimize designs, reduce costs, and accelerate time-to-market. Ansys goes beyond its industry-leading simulation software and actively contributes to the open-source Python community through the PyAnsys project. By harnessing the capabilities of Ansys within a Python environment, PyAnsys offers engineers and researchers a seamless experience, automating and streamlining processes for faster design iterations. From structural analysis to fluid dynamics and electromagnetics, PyAnsys provides many user-friendly Python packages for preparing, solving, and post-processing simulations. With PyAnsys, engineers can tap into Ansys' simulation prowess while enjoying the flexibility and efficiency of Python programming, enabling them to unlock new levels of productivity and innovation.

  • Posit Software, PBC helps people understand and improve the world through data. We build tools that enable robust and reproducible data analysis through programming, paired with tools that make it easy to share insights. Our core software is open source, freely available to anyone. Our professional software equips individuals and teams to develop and share their work at scale.

  • Formed in 2003, Mozilla exists at the nexus of non-profit - meets tech company - meets open source advocacy group. We’re committed to keeping the internet an open resource, accessible to all and broadening its accessibility to underserved communities across the globe. We build products, tools, and services to enable this. We’re also passionate about ensuring that users maintain agency over their identities online, with user privacy and security being at the core of our products. From a Data perspective, this presents us with unique challenges of continuing to innovate and adapt while also embracing Lean Data Practices (LDP) - being more thoughtful about the data we collect so that we can drive meaningful impact while collecting less data overall.

    Learn more at www.mozilla.org

  • Advances in hybrid cloud, AI and quantum – and a deepening understanding of how they will come together to enable unprecedented new capabilities to accelerate science, discovery and impact – have significant potential to accelerate the rate of discovering solutions to urgent problems our clients, industries and society at large are facing. At IBM Research, we are bringing advancements across these cutting-edge technologies together to accelerate discovery of solutions across a range of challenges including future pandemics, climate change, drug design and more sustainable materials. We invite you to learn more about our mission to accelerate discovery.

    Open-Source Science (OSSci) is a community initiative at NumFOCUS, the home of leading Open-Source Software for science in general and data science in particular. OSSci is focused on three things: (1) Community Stewardship for OSS projects serving science; (2) mapping all of science areas onto OSS projects used in those areas, prioritizing needs and gaps; (3) resourcing the work required to fill in the gaps. OSSci is cofounded by IBM Research and partners from academia, industry, government, and startups. OSSci operates as a set of interest groups in science verticals (materials, healthcare, climate) and technology horizontals (reproducibility, data formats, scalability).

  • Dask and Coiled make it easy to scale Python to run in parallel. Dask provides parallel algorithms for PyData and Coiled provides distributed infrastructure in the cloud.

Through collaboration, providing resources and building technology, our mission is to help build a more inclusive, just and healthy future for everyone. For more information, please visit chanzuckerberg.com.
